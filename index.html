Privacy Architecture

ProofPass never stores sensitive personal information directly onchain. Instead, it utilizes cryptographic commitments: Commitment = Hash(Secret || Data).

For age verification, we implement a genuine cryptographic hash chain. The issuer hashes a random seed Age times and commits the result. To prove age ≥ 18, the holder reveals the seed hashed (Age − 18) times; the verifier hashes it 18 more times and compares it to the on-chain commitment. Someone under 18 cannot produce that value.
